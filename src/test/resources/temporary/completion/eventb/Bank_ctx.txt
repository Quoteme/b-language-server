=== #7bf5c165a021
```eventb
s ∘ r
```

---

**Relational backward composition** · Typed as `circ`.

An element x is related by r ;S to an element y if there is an element z such that r relates x to z and S relates z to y.

s ∘ r can be written as an alternative to r ;s. This reflects the fact that f(g(x)) = (f ∘ g)(x) holds for two functions f and g.

The relational overwrite r s is equal to r except all entries in r whose first element is in the domain of s are replaced by the corresponding entries in s.

The parallel product r | s relates a pair x ↦ y to a pair m ↦ n when r relates x to m and s relates y to n.

If a relation r relates an element x to y and s relates x to z, the direct product r ⊗ s relates x to the pair y ↦ z.

The inverse relation r^-1 relates an element x to y if the original relation r relates y to x.

*Definition:* r ∘ s ≙ s ;r  
*Type:* r ∘ s ∈ ℙ(γ × α) with r ∈ ℙ(α × β) and s ∈ ℙ(β × γ)  
*Well-definedness:* L(r ∘ s) ≙ L(r) ∧ L(s)

**Example**
```eventb
{2 ↦ 3} ∘ {1 ↦ 2} = {1 ↦ 3}
```

[Rodin Handbook, 3.3.5.5 Operations on relations](https://www3.hhu.de/stups/handbook/rodin/current/html/relations.html#operations_on_relations)

---

**Partitions of a set** · Typed as `partition`.

partition(S,s1,…,sn) is a predicate that states that the sets s1,…,sn constitute a partition of S. The union of all elements of a partition is S and all elements are disjoint.

partition(S) is equivalent to S = ∅ and partition(S,s) to S = s.

*Definition:* partition(S,s1,…,sn) ≙ S = s1 ∪ … ∪ sn ∧ ∀ i,j · i ≠ j ⇒ si ∩ sj = ∅  
*Type:* partition(S,s1,…,sn) is a predicate with S ∈ ℙ(α) and si ∈ ℙ(α) for i ∈ 1‥n  
*Well-definedness:* L(partition(S,s1,…,sn)) ≙ L(S) ∧ L(s1) ∧ … ∧ L(sn)

**Example**
```eventb
partition(1 ‥ 4, {1, 2}, {3, 4})
```
